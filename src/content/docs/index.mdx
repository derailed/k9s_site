K9s is a terminal based UI to interact with your Kubernetes clusters. The aim of this project is to make it easier to navigate, observe and manage your deployed applications in the wild. K9s continually watches Kubernetes for changes and offers subsequent commands to interact with your observed resources.

## Features

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Information At Your Finger Tips" icon="information">
    Tracks in real-time the activities of resources running in your Kubernetes
    cluster.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Standard or CRD?" icon="document">
    Handles both Kubernetes standard resources as well as custom resource
    definitions.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Cluster Metrics" icon="bars">
    Tracks real-time metrics associated with resources such as pods, containers
    and nodes.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Power Users Welcome" icon="rocket">
    Standard cluster management commands such as logs, scaling, port-forwards
    and restarts, plus custom aliases, hotkeys, plugins and powerful filtering.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Error Zoom" icon="warning">
    Drill down directly to what's wrong with your cluster's resources.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Skinnable and Customizable" icon="puzzle">
    Define your very own look and feel via K9s skins and arrange which columns
    to display on a per resource basis.
  </Card>
  <Card title="MultiResource Views" icon="seti:default">
    Provides an overview of your cluster resources via Pulses and XRay views.
  </Card>
  <Card title="We've Got Your RBAC" icon="approve-check">
    View RBAC rules such as cluster/roles and their bindings, and reverse-lookup
    what a user, group or ServiceAccount can do.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Built-in Benchmarking" icon="bars">
    Benchmark your HTTP services and pods directly from K9s to tune your
    resource requests and limits.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Resource Graph Traversals" icon="random">
    Easily traverse Kubernetes resources and their associated resources.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
